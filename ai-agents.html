What is an AI agent in marketing?

An AI agent is goal-driven software that leverages large language models (LLMs) paired with external tools—such as Google Search Console, Screaming Frog APIs, web scrapers, and your CMS—to autonomously complete multi-step marketing operations like auditing technical health, tracking competitors, and generating data-backed briefs.
