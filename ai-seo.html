AI SEO SERVICES

Ranking first means nothing if the answer above you never names you

Most sites are still optimised for ten blue links that fewer people scroll to every month. AI writes one answer and cites three sources. Here’s where an AI SEO consultant actually moves the needle.

Entity SEO & Brand Identity

Models cite entities they can resolve with confidence. Consistent naming, knowledge graph signals, author identity, and the co-occurrence patterns that tie your brand to your category.

Schema Architecture

Not one Article block bolted onto a template. A connected graph linked with @id references so machines can traverse your site and resolve what you are and what you sell.

Content Structured for Extraction

AI hits passages, not pages. Self-contained chunks, answers up front, and definitional sentences that still make sense when pulled out of context.

Query Fan-Out Research

One prompt becomes dozens of hidden sub-queries before an answer is written. I map the fan-out for your category and build coverage your primary keyword tool never showed you.

Third-Party Citation Sources

Reddit, YouTube, G2, industry roundups and comparison posts feed the models. I get your brand placed and described correctly in the sources they already trust.

AI Visibility Tracking

Prompt sets re-run across six platforms every month. Share of voice, sentiment, which competitor got named instead of you, and the exact URLs that earned the citation.

It’s crucial because a growing share of buying research now ends inside an answer that names three companies. If you aren’t one of them, the click never happens, no matter where you rank.

They are closely overlapping terms for the same shift. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on winning direct answer snippets. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and LLMO focus on generative synthesis inside ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. I treat them as one unified discipline: getting your brand extracted and recommended by AI.

We run recurring test suites of 100 to 300 intent-driven buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude. We measure Share of Voice (SoV), entity placement sentiment, and track direct referral traffic and downstream conversions via Google Analytics 4.

Perplexity and Google AI Overviews fetch live web pages and can cite optimized passages in as little as 2 to 4 weeks. Base model weights (like standard ChatGPT releases) take longer as training and fine-tuning cycles occur over several months.
