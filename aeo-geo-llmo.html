What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the strategic practice of optimizing your content architecture, technical schema, and brand signals so generative AI platforms (such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity) comprehend, trust, and directly cite your business in their synthesized answers.
