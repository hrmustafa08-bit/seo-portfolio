SEO CONSULTING FOR ECOMMERCE

SEO consulting for ecommerce: what I do

I am an independent SEO consultant for ecommerce brands. The work starts with how search engines and buyers move through your store, then focuses on the pages that sell.

Technical audit

Crawling, indexation, faceted navigation, canonical loops, status codes, and redirects. Checked against your real catalog. The result is a short, ranked list of what stops catalog pages from ranking.

Category and product page SEO

Titles, headings, copy, internal links, and filters on the pages buyers land on. For large catalogs, I set repeatable templates so thousands of products are not indexed blind.

Schema and structured data

Product, offer, review, and breadcrumb markup that matches what buyers see on the page, so search engines and AI models read price, availability, and variations correctly.

Internal linking and URLs

A category structure that passes authority to products you want to rank, and a plan for variant, parameter, and filter URLs. Here is how to handle duplicate collection URLs.

International and AI search visibility

Hreflang and URL architecture for stores that sell in several countries or languages, plus product and brand information that AI answers can quote directly.

Reporting

Search Console and GA4 reporting by landing page and page type, so you see what shipped, what moved, and what comes next, without vanity dashboards.

Who this is for

  • Store owners and ecommerce managers on Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, or BigCommerce or a custom build.
  • Stores with a large catalog, many variants, or filters that create duplicate or thin URLs.
  • Teams planning a replatform or migration who want rankings protected through the move.
  • Brands selling in several countries or languages.
  • In-house teams that need a senior specialist to audit, set priorities, and support developers.
Not the best fit
  • Anyone looking for guaranteed rankings or bulk cheap backlinks.
  • Search results depend on your site, market, and code quality, so I do not promise vanity outcomes.
  • A single-location business without an online catalog.
