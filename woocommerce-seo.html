WOOCOMMERCE SEO

Every WooCommerce store has the same SEO leaks

WooCommerce inherits all of WordPress’s flexibility, and its SEO caveats. These are the six places I see WooCommerce stores lose rankings and revenue most often. I fix each one.

Permalinks & Indexation

Product and category URL structures, pagination crawl traps, and tag archives that split your ranking signals across duplicate pages.

Core Web Vitals & Speed

WooCommerce speed lives and dies by theme, plugins, and hosting. I tune LCP, INP, and CLS with caching and script control so pages load fast.

Plugin & Script Bloat

Every plugin adds weight. I audit your plugin footprint, cut render-blocking scripts, and keep only what earns its place on the page.

Product & Category Schema

Product, Offer, Review, and BreadcrumbList markup done right, including variable products, prices, and stock states, so listings earn rich results and AI citations.

Faceted Filters

WooCommerce filters can spawn thousands of near-duplicate URLs. I set canonical, noindex, and crawl rules so filters help shoppers, not search engines.

Yoast & Rank Math Tuning

The right plugin, configured correctly. Titles, canonicals, sitemaps, and breadcrumbs set so nothing leaks and every template is covered.
